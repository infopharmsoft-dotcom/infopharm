📋 تفاصيل الدواء

Ceftriaxone is a broad-spectrum third-generation cephalosporin antibiotic with strong activity against Gram-negative and some Gram-positive bacteria. It works by inhibiting bacterial cell wall synthesis through binding to penicillin-binding proteins (PBPs). This leads to defective cell wall formation and ultimately bacterial cell lysis and death (bactericidal effect). It has a long half-life, allowing once-daily dosing in many infections. Indications include respiratory tract infections, urinary tract infections, sepsis, meningitis, and intra-abdominal infections.
